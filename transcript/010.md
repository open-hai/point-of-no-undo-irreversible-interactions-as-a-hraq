## What I found

**Paper:** Rossmy, Terzimehić, Döring, Buschek & Wiethoff, *Point of no Undo: Irreversible Interactions as a Design Strategy*, CHI '23 art. 140 — a speculative design paper built on three artifacts: *4 on the Floor* (Connect-Four step sequencer), *SocialShredder* (likes shred your Polaroid), *Punishable AI* (break a robot's legs as feedback).

**Verdict: partial — 7 reproduced, 6 partial, 2 blocked, across 15 inner-loop components.**

The decisive find was in the artifact hunt: the paper releases nothing, but `github.com/BeatRossmy/PunishableAI` (MIT) exists — firmware published under the *earlier* DIS '20 paper, never referenced here, found by searching GitHub for the artifact's name. Porting `spider.ino` to Python let me actually check the paper against an artifact, which produced the two most useful findings:

- The paper says leg state "could be sensed" through interrupted PCB traces. The released `loop()` configures pins 2–7 and **never reads them**; with three legs broken the commanded servo angles are byte-identical to the intact robot. → **blocked**, contradiction.
- The three-step escalation (scold → flashlight → break) is graded for the participant, not by the robot: light and touch call the same `jiggle()`, and scolding has no code path.
- The firmware also settles two things the paper never states — six legs, and that "getting off track" is `random(0,3)`.

Two arithmetic mismatches came out of `src/consistency.py`: *4 on the Floor*'s 8 + 6 + 2 groups account for 16 of 20 participants, and *SocialShredder*'s theme counts sum to 44 over N=16 with no denominator ever stated.

**Boundary calls I defended explicitly:** the physical artifacts are *inner* (hardware is expensive, not human — the control logic is the reproducible substance); the Section 4 conceptualisation is *outer* (its warrant is participant behaviour), but its self-consistency — taxonomy coverage, the empty "close & delayed" cell, count arithmetic — is inner and was checked.

**Everything runs:** `python -m src.run_all` exits 0; chip tracking 64/64 cells, a real MIDI file, the shred invariant (un-liking restores the like, never the photo), the gait port, and the two consistency audits. The one number the paper reports (medians 2.5 vs 27.5) is **blocked** — no data anywhere — so `src/analyze.py` exists as the contract in `instrument.json` and has only ever run on clearly-labelled synthetic filler, which I say in three places so it can't be mistaken for a result.
